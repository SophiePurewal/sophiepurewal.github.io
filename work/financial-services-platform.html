Commercial UI design case study

Supporting the launch of a high-traffic financial-services platform

UI refinement, component redesign and UAT during the final delivery phase of a large regulated website.

I joined the project during its later delivery phase, when the website’s core structure and journeys were already established. My role was to strengthen the final interface quality, redesign selected components and support UAT ahead of release.

My work included refining parts of the salary-sacrifice calculator, reviewing responsive behaviour and visual consistency, documenting design and usability issues, and working with the wider team to verify fixes.

Project summary

Role
Senior UI Designer
Project stage
Late-stage delivery and launch support
My contribution
Component redesign, salary-sacrifice calculator UI, design-quality assurance and UAT
Environment
Regulated financial services, responsive web and cross-functional delivery
Service context
A well-established, high-traffic financial-services website with sustained usage across multiple years

Context and challenge

The project was already well advanced when I joined. The challenge was therefore not to redefine the entire product, but to improve selected areas while working within an established design system, delivery plan and technical implementation.

This required quickly understanding the existing patterns, identifying inconsistencies and usability risks, and making focused improvements without disrupting the wider service.

What I owned

  • UI refinement of selected production components
  • Redesign of parts of the salary-sacrifice calculator
  • Responsive layout and component review
  • Visual consistency against established design-system patterns
  • Review of interaction states and error handling
  • Identification and documentation of UAT issues
  • Collaboration with product, engineering and delivery colleagues
  • Verification of fixes before release

Salary-sacrifice calculator

The salary-sacrifice calculator was one of the main areas I contributed to.

The salary-sacrifice calculator presented complex financial information and required a clear hierarchy between user inputs, supporting guidance and calculated outcomes. I refined selected parts of the interface to make the flow easier to follow and more consistent with the wider design system.

  • Clearer visual hierarchy
  • More consistent spacing and alignment
  • Improved grouping of related inputs
  • Clearer distinction between editable fields and calculated outputs
  • Review of responsive behaviour
  • Review of validation and interaction states
  • Alignment with existing component patterns

UAT and design-quality assurance

A significant part of my contribution involved reviewing the implemented website during UAT. I assessed the interface across pages and screen sizes, comparing the live implementation with the intended design and identifying issues that could affect clarity, consistency or usability.

  1. Review the implemented page or component
  2. Compare it with the approved design and established UI patterns
  3. Record the issue clearly with supporting context
  4. Prioritise issues based on user impact and severity
  5. Discuss fixes with the relevant delivery colleagues
  6. Recheck the implementation after changes were made

This work helped protect final design quality during delivery, without implying that every defect across the website was found or resolved.

Senior UI design judgement

Joining late in delivery required a different kind of design judgement from leading an early-stage concept. I needed to understand the existing system quickly, distinguish between critical issues and lower-priority polish, and recommend improvements that were realistic within the remaining delivery constraints.

Working within constraints

Improving the interface without destabilising established journeys or delivery commitments.

Prioritising user impact

Focusing attention on clarity, interaction states, responsive behaviour and areas involving complex financial information.

Collaborating through delivery

Communicating issues clearly and working with product and engineering colleagues to reach practical resolutions.

Outcome

My contribution supported the final stages of delivery by improving selected production components, strengthening parts of the salary-sacrifice calculator experience and helping the team identify and resolve UI issues during UAT.

The project demonstrates my ability to contribute effectively within an established product environment, work carefully with regulated financial content and maintain design quality during a pressured delivery phase.

What I learned

  • Late-stage design work requires strong prioritisation
  • UAT is an important part of product-design quality
  • Small component decisions can significantly affect consistency
  • Clear issue documentation improves collaboration with engineering
  • Regulated financial interfaces require particular care around hierarchy, guidance and interaction states
