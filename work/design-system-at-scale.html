Challenge

Creating consistency across complex product journeys

Multiple product teams needed accessible, reusable UI patterns that could support complex digital services without repeatedly solving the same interface problems. Fragmented legacy patterns and evolving requirements created inconsistency across forms, navigation, feedback and layout.

My work focused on modernising shared components, defining their behaviour and states, progressing them through design and engineering governance, and helping teams adopt them confidently.

My contribution

Designing components and supporting adoption

  • Audited and modernised shared UI components
  • Defined responsive behaviour, variants and interaction states
  • Improved accessibility across form and feedback patterns
  • Collaborated with designers and engineers through governance and release
  • Created documentation and supported adoption through practical guidance

Governance

Moving components from audit to adoption

Components were treated as shared product infrastructure rather than isolated design assets. Fifteen redesigned Core Components progressed through peer-design and engineering review before release into the shared library.

Outcome

A more reusable and measurable design foundation

Over 12 months, the components I built and modernised recorded more than 21,000 Figma-tracked insertions across header, status, feedback, layout and form patterns.

The adoption data demonstrates more than component production: it shows reuse across files and teams, supported by governance, documentation, engineering collaboration and adoption measurement.
