Overview

Giving a local campaign a clear, credible online home

This self-initiated project began with an idea I conceived to give the Jarvis Brook Skatepark extension campaign a stronger public voice. The ageing park and limited space no longer meet the needs of its growing wheeled-sports community, so I created a compelling online destination that could explain the approved plans, build confidence and turn interest into practical and financial support.

The live experience

Designed around the energy of the park

The visual direction draws from skate culture and the park’s existing community artwork. The page leads visitors from the current challenge to the approved vision, fundraising activity and clear ways to help.

My role

Conceiving and delivering the campaign from scratch

I conceived the idea, defined the campaign story and designed and built the complete website from the ground up. I used an AI prompt-to-prototype workflow to accelerate implementation while retaining ownership of the concept, user experience, visual direction, content and final quality.

  • Conceived the idea and defined the campaign proposition and narrative
  • Created the information architecture, UX and responsive UI design
  • Built the website from scratch through AI-assisted prompting and prototyping
  • Planned and produced all photography and video used across the experience
  • Created the visual identity, art direction and campaign content
  • Worked with local authorities, councils, charities and community stakeholders to bring the project together
  • Led accessibility, testing, refinement and launch

Design approach

Turning community interest into action

  • Lead with the emotional value of the park before introducing plans and fundraising details
  • Use authentic photography and the park’s bright visual character to keep the campaign rooted in its community
  • Create a clear narrative from the current problem to the proposed future
  • Make support actions prominent, specific and easy to find
  • Use AI prompt-to-prototype methods to move quickly while reviewing and refining every design and implementation decision
  • Build responsive layouts, readable contrast and keyboard-accessible interactions into the experience

Outcome

A shareable destination for the campaign

The campaign now has a live, mobile-friendly website that brings the extension plans, fundraising activity and ways to contribute together in one coherent experience. It provides local families, riders, funders, councils, charities and community partners with a shared destination for understanding and supporting the project.
