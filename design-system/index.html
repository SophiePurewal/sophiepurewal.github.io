Dude, Where’s My Bike? Design System

A complete design system and interactive product prototype for a bike-hire concept.

Starting with an initial bike-hire product concept, I developed its visual language, reusable components and core interaction patterns into a complete design system. I then applied the system across the product prototype to create a consistent, accessible and scalable experience.
