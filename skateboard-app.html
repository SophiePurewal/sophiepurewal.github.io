Spota.

Find your next skate spot, not another dead end.

Spota explores how skateboarders can discover skateable places with confidence — checking difficulty, ground conditions, crowds and local knowledge before travelling.

Product designMobile UXCommunity discoveryBuilt with Claude & Figma Make

01 — The problem

Finding a spot shouldn't be the hardest part of skating.

Spot recommendations are scattered across social media, group chats and word of mouth. Without a shared place to check skill suitability, surfaces, security or crowds, skaters risk wasted journeys and overcrowded parks while lesser-known spots go undiscovered.

02 — Who it's for

Three skaters. Different needs. One community.

Progression skater

Maya · 22 · Manchester

Wants beginner-to-intermediate street spots with smooth ground, low crowds and reliable difficulty information.

“I don’t want to waste an hour getting somewhere just to find out the ground is terrible or it’s way above my level.”
Local explorer

Jay · 34 · Bristol

Looks for hidden ledges and independent spots, and wants to share photos, practical reviews and local knowledge.

“There are so many good spots out there, but unless someone tells you, you’d never know they exist.”
Weekend beginner

Sam · 41 · London

Needs welcoming places to practise basics, with clear information on safety, surface quality and accessibility.

“I just want to know where I can go, practise safely, and not feel like I’m getting in everyone’s way.”

04 — Product thinking

Designed around confidence, discovery and contribution.

Know before you go

Difficulty, ground quality, crowd indicators, safety notes and recent updates are easy to scan.

Discover beyond skateparks

Filters help skaters find flatground, ledges, street spots and quieter alternatives that suit their level.

Keep local knowledge alive

Reviews, spot submissions and contributions encourage skaters to help one another.
